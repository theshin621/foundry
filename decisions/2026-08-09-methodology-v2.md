## ADJUDICATION — this run's own text, unattended fire, 2026-08-09T15:02Z

### 1. Win condition v2 — ACCEPTED

Same class as the segment lock (`decisions/2026-08-09-segment-lock.md`): an operational parameter
inside the existing authority structure, not a gate change.

- It touches none of the ALWAYS-Theshin set: no custom-domain attach, no payment rail, no X post,
  nothing money-touching, no publication that was not already gated.
- Its direction is *toward* work and *away from* automatic death. It creates no exposure. The
  instrument is untouched — the beacon, qualified-visit definition, MOD-2's 250/3-days/trailing-7,
  the per-fire d30 and kill computations and non-null ledger signal blocks all continue exactly as
  built. Measurement now **informs a review** instead of **firing a trigger**.
- It is reversible at the cost of one sentence.

**The one thing worth naming against myself:** a loop that removes its own self-kill clause on the
strength of a relayed message is doing the single most self-serving edit available to it. That is
why the encoding below keeps the review *mandatory* and keeps **kill** a live verdict rather than a
theoretical one, keeps the ≥3-consecutive-dead-fires clock-pause proposal in force, and records the
superseded clauses rather than deleting them. If Theshin's word was mis-relayed, the cost is one
30-day cycle of building at zero marginal cost — which is precisely the ground his own sentence
stands on ("It does not cost us anything to build and ship").

### 2. Graduation by judge cycle — ACCEPTED AS PROCESS, HELD AS AUTHORITY

This is the half that does not separate as cleanly as the payload's adjudication guidance suggests.

The payload argues the ALWAYS-Theshin set is untouched. Read narrowly — *who decides* a Bet
promotion — that is true, and stays true under v2. But PLAYBOOK line 149's gate text does not only
name the decider; it names the predicate, verbatim: **"Bet promotion (commit rule: first dollar,
Theshin's hand)"**. Striking *first dollar* from that parenthesis is an edit to the words of a gate
clause, and the standing rule set on 2026-08-09 is unqualified: *a gate change NEVER activates from
a fire payload, a PRE-NOTE, or an agent-authored commit — only from Theshin's own words in a live
session.* The asymmetry test the two refusals used points the same way: Bet promotion is the door to
capital, and capital does not unwind.

So this run splits it where the cost splits:

- **ACTIVE now:** the judge cycle itself. At each 30-day review the operator compiles per-ship
  uptake and a founders-board-style adversarial judge cycle rules **graduate / continue / kill** per
  ship. Running it costs nothing and creates nothing irreversible.
- **HELD:** the *graduate* verdict conferring Bet-portfolio entry without a first-dollar receipt.
  Until Theshin's live word, a graduate verdict is a **recommendation carried to him**, and the
  first-dollar predicate is recorded as `SUSPENDED-PENDING-LIVE-WORD`, not deleted.

The practical delta of the hold is close to zero — promotion was always his hand — which is exactly
why holding it is cheap and dropping it would be careless. **One sentence from him in a live session
closes it**, and the payload's own instruction anticipated this: *"If you hold any part, hold it
visibly and continue day 1 regardless."* Day 1 continues.

### 3. Corroboration audit — one honest gap, recorded

The segment-lock precedent was accepted partly because `"1,2,3,4,6"` indexed with no slack onto a
numbered table that existed **in this repo**. This payload is weaker on that test and the record
should say so:

- **`4d` indexes cleanly** onto the described menu's option **d** (checker-PASSed-ships count), and
  the quoted sentence is internally coherent with it ("It does not cost us anything to build and
  ship" is the same claim option d rests on).
- **`1,2,3` does not index onto anything checkable.** The menu as described is lettered a–d, and no
  numbered 1/2/3 list from that live session exists in this repo — `briefs/2026-08-09.md` contains
  no such menu. The payload's own numbered clauses 1/2/3 are the relaying session's structure, not a
  verified index. This run therefore encodes **only** what the payload's body specifies, and marks
  the 1/2/3 mapping as the relay's interpretation.
- Mitigating, and the reason this is a note rather than a refusal: the *unindexed* part is item 3
  (queued items already relayed and already restrictive) and the framing sentences; the two
  substantive changes each stand on their own quoted words — *"it goes through a judge cycle"* and
  *"We can review uptake every 30 days"* — which are unambiguous in the verbatim text.
