### Build order (the recorded constraint)

1. **The door carries TWO line caps, not one.** `maxLines` bounds NON-BLANK lines
   (downstream work). A new `maxSegments` bounds TOTAL segments — the quantity
   `String.split` actually produces, and therefore the quantity the preprocessing
   loop between the door and the budget iterates over. Yesterday's fix excluded
   blank lines from the single cap, which cured a real off-by-one and simultaneously
   let ~1,000,000 blank segments through into an unmetered `split()` + per-line trim
   (measured by the re-checker at 1,309–1,342 ms reported, 16–34 s wall clock, on a
   fully door-legal input). Binding rule going into `lib/door.js`: **no raw split may
   be taken over a quantity the door has not bounded.**
2. **The re-entrancy boolean becomes a generation counter.** `running` is a lock with
   no release path on the re-entrant case: an input arriving while a prior run's
   post-paint measurement is pending is silently dropped and the page can strand on
   "working…" forever while `data-state` reads `idle`. Replace with a monotonic
   generation where a stale generation's completion is discarded and every release of
   the in-flight slot re-pumps if the newest generation is unpainted — so it cannot
   deadlock and cannot drop the latest answer.
3. **Re-derive the printed timing range from measurement**, because (1) changes the
   worst case and the page prints a number it must be able to keep.
4. **Start from `lib/checks/door-limits.json` + `lib/checks/codeowners-oracle.json`.**
   Both already carry the probes and the regression notes; the matcher is not touched
   because it was never the defect.
