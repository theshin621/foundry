## NEXT — the rebuild constraint for tomorrow

Both new defects are small and precise, and both are the *same shape* as the original root
cause — which is why they are a design constraint, not a patch list:

1. **The door must carry two line caps, not one.** Non-blank lines bound *downstream work*;
   *total segments* bound the **preprocessing** between the door and the budget. Excluding
   blank lines from the single cap fixed the off-by-one and simultaneously let ~1 M blank
   segments through into an unmetered `split()` + per-line trim. Rule: the door must bound
   every quantity any loop between the door and the budget will iterate over, and no raw split
   may be taken over a quantity the door has not bounded.
2. **Replace the re-entrancy boolean with a generation counter.** `running` is a lock with no
   release path on the re-entrant case, so a run can be dropped and the page stranded on
   "working…" with `data-state` idle. A monotonic generation, where a stale generation's
   completion is discarded and the newest input always schedules a run, cannot deadlock and
   cannot drop the latest answer.

Everything else is inherited proven: 171/171, 13/13, 6/6 against the live page in both rounds;
~55–66 XSS positions with zero injections; byte-identical build reproducibility; cookieless,
two requests, no storage; mobile clean.
